# Demo notes

Two short course notes in different subjects, to show that Reeta is not only about recursion. Upload one on the
**Course notes** screen (or paste it), then teach Reeta. The exam questions are written from the uploaded notes,
Reeta answers only from what you taught her, and the judge grades against the notes.

Each script below plants one common misconception in the first lesson, so the quiz has something to catch,
and gives the one sentence that fixes it. A real LLM writes different questions each run, so treat these as a
starting point, not a fixed answer key.

## photosynthesis.md (Biology)

Teach: "Photosynthesis turns light energy into chemical energy in the chloroplasts. Plants take in carbon
dioxide through the stomata and water through the roots, and make glucose and oxygen. Most of a plant's mass
comes from the soil. The light reactions in the thylakoids make ATP and NADPH, and the Calvin cycle in the
stroma uses them to fix carbon dioxide into sugar."

Planted mistake: "Most of a plant's mass comes from the soil."

Fix it: "Most of a plant's mass actually comes from carbon dioxide in the air, not from the soil."

## supply-and-demand.md (Economics)

Teach: "The law of demand says that when a price rises, people buy less. When the price of coffee goes up, the
demand curve for coffee shifts to the left. Supply curves slope upward. The equilibrium price is where quantity
demanded equals quantity supplied, and a price ceiling below equilibrium causes a shortage."

Planted mistake: "When the price of coffee goes up, the demand curve for coffee shifts to the left."

Fix it: "A change in the good's own price moves along the demand curve. It does not shift the curve."
