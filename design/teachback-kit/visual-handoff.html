Implementation notes

The details that make it feel like a game.

ElementDesign rule
Chalkboard12px walnut frame, deep green fill, pale chalk. Chalk dust, old wipe streaks, a chalk stick and a felt eraser on the tray. Keep textures behind the text.
NotebookWarm paper, 33px rules, red left margin, Caveat handwriting, small rotations, scribbles and crossed-out drafts. Number entries. Cross-outs are illustrative drafts, not stored evidence. Put source sentence and timestamp on an expandable note footer.
Red penDraw the mark in 250ms, then reveal the correction. Keep exam text upright; rotate only the short annotation by −2°.
Evidence linkClick or keyboard-select a wrong question to highlight its note and source sentence together. Use matching IDs and amber fill plus an underline. Scroll to offscreen evidence.
Missing evidenceIf nothing was taught for a concept, show “Not covered in your lecture.” Do not invent a source sentence.
VoiceReady: Start lecture. Recording: timer + Stop lecture + visible recording label. Processing: Transcribing lecture… Failure: retain the recording and offer Retry. Then show the transcript for review.
Responsive layoutDesktop: board left, notebook right, exam below. Under 740px: board → student → notebook → exam. Keep one primary action per phase.
Accessibility44px targets, visible focus, text labels with ×/✓, selectable transcript, live status. Respect reduced motion. No flashing recording indicators.
Visual prototype only. The buttons demonstrate evidence highlighting and a re-teaching scene; they do not record audio, transcribe, or grade an exam.
